My Schooling

Despite being incredibly young, I am currently going to Tiffin University through online classes. I am able to take these classes thanks to the CCP program that lets students in grades 7–12 take college classes and have them count for both college and high school. I have been taking these classes since the summer between my 7th and 8th grade year. Thanks to starting these classes so early and the fact that they count for both high school and college, my current plan is to graduate college a year before what was originally supposed to be my high school graduation year. In my future, after I finish school, I plan to go into AI development.
